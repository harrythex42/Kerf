ALDER SYSTEMS / DELIVERY

Customer portal launch retrospective

June 2026 · Delivery review for Product, Support and Customer Success

What shipped

Customers can now see renewal dates, retrieve invoices and nominate billing contacts without opening a support ticket. The rollout began with eight pilot accounts and expanded to all eligible accounts after the Thursday readiness review.

Early adoption

Within two weeks, 210 account administrators had accessed the portal. Invoice downloads accounted for most sessions. The billing-contact change flow was used less often, but the requests it replaced were usually the ones that took Support the longest to resolve.

How we released

The team kept the billing system as the source of truth. Product could pause new access without disabling the portal for pilot accounts, and Support retained the existing request path throughout the rollout.

What slowed us down

The invitation template assumed the administrator had already signed in. On trial accounts that assumption did not hold, so the first invite led to a generic error. The team fixed the link and asked pilot participants to repeat the critical first-use task.

What we would keep

A shared rollout log kept the review short. Support wrote down the actual customer wording, and Engineering attached the matching audit event. The two views made it possible to distinguish a product defect from a training question.
