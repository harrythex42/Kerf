NORTHLINE SUPPLY / OPERATIONS

Q2 operations review

East and Central warehouse network · April–June 2026 · Prepared for the operating review

Quarter in brief

Northline completed 18,420 outbound orders during the quarter, up 7% from Q1. On-time dispatch reached 96.8%, just below the 97% target. The remaining gap was concentrated in the last East pickup window, when late inbound cartons also compete for staging space.

The network absorbed the increase without adding a weekend shift. Central took two weekly replenishment routes from East, which freed the East team to clear held orders before the final handover. The route change remains temporary until Finance confirms the fuel and handling impact in July.

Service and inventory

Stock records agreed with the June cycle count for 98.9% of audited lines. The discrepancies were mostly split cases registered as whole cartons. Supervisors now record both quantities when serialised stock is transferred to the returns lane.

Supplier lead times improved for standard packaging, but the spring catalogue introduced three items whose protective inserts must be made to order. Purchasing has agreed to keep six weeks of inserts on hand until the first after-season reconciliation.

People and equipment

Two new shift leads completed the handover checklist. The East label printer was replaced after a second feeder failure, and the retired unit is being held for parts. The spare handheld scanners moved to a shared pool with a sign-out sheet.

Dispatch handover

Dispatch now uses an exception queue rather than passing a marked-up pick list between shifts. A carton is not released until its label, carrier and lane agree with the booking. A failed check is assigned to one owner before the next pickup window.

July decision

Operations recommends holding the route split through July and reviewing the last pickup window independently. If East misses the service target for two consecutive weeks, trial an earlier supplier cutoff before adding a shift.
