BRACKEN INSTRUMENTS / FIELD SUPPORT

Field reader deployment guide

Model FR-2 · Site acceptance and service checks · Revision 3

Before the visit

The FR-2 records environmental sensor readings and stores them until the site gateway acknowledges receipt. Before the visit, confirm the mounting position, the sensor list and the gateway identifier with the site coordinator.

Kit and access

Bring the reader, its labelled power supply, a bracket, a spare sensor lead and a printed site schedule. On arrival, check that the work area is dry and that the facilities contact can be reached if the mounting position changes.

Leave the old reader connected until the replacement has reported its first test reading. Use the service reference, not the building name alone, when opening a maintenance record; some sites share the same name.

Commissioning

Register the reader against the gateway before attaching production sensors. A newly registered unit reports a test value every thirty seconds. Use the portal timestamp to check that a reading was accepted after installation.

When a reading does not arrive

Check the gateway identifier against the work order and look for a recent heartbeat. If the heartbeat is current but the value is missing, replace the sensor lead and repeat the test before replacing the reader.

Service handover

Give the coordinator the completed checklist and explain where to look for the most recent reading. At the first service review, compare the portal’s event log with the worksheet and close any actions that have supporting evidence.
